## <Icon icon="sparkles" /> Get a key

Buy a plan or the paid 10-day trial at [nablr.dev](https://nablr.dev). Your key arrives by email.

## <Icon icon="key" /> Activate a paid key

After buying, you receive a key by email (`sk_nablr_...`).

```
Call set_license_key with sk_nablr_<your-key-here>
```

The key is validated against the license server and cached locally. Subsequent calls use the in-memory cache - no network round-trip on every prompt.

## <Icon icon="life-buoy" /> Recover a lost key

Email `support@nablr.dev` or use the recovery endpoint - your key is re-sent to the email address used at signup.

## <Icon icon="users" /> Team plan

Team plans share a single key across up to 5 devices. The 6th device is rejected with a `device_limit_reached` error. Contact `support@nablr.dev` to rotate a device slot.

## <Icon icon="wifi-off" /> Airgap environments

After activating a key, paid assets are pre-cached locally. Subsequent prompt generation makes zero network calls - fully functional in restricted network environments.

See the [Security](/security) page for firewall allowlist details.
